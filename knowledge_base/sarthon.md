# Sarthon

Sarthon is one of Nelvar's legendary Triad of Veils. With a life-ambition to learn all of the world's secrets, Sarthon seeks immortality so that he might live all of the lives necessary to accomplish his task. After being caught red-handed performing unethical experiments on his fellow citizens for the sake of this immortality, Sarthon defected from Nelvar rather than be persecuted for his ambitions, and for many years sought the village's destruction in order to take revenge and demonstrate what he had learned. After realising the flaws of his approach in life from witnessing it emulated by Erlan Vestor, his former associate, Sarthon began focusing his research on the development of life rather than its manipulation after seeing the actions of his former apprentice, Veylan Morvek.
