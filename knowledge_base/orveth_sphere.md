# Orveth Sphere

The Orveth Sphere is a weaving that takes form as a spinning ball of Mavren, invented by Davor Elsen.

Davor came up with the idea for this technique after seeing a Vorath pulse being used. Because his lover, Seyra Elvor, was a vessel-bearer, she would inevitably be deployed to fight opponents who would use Vorath pulses against her. Davor therefore wanted to develop a technique that Seyra could use to match against those opponents, which also worked with her Mavren volume as a vessel-bearer. Davor struggled with the original concepts for the technique, as they were either too weak or too unstable for use.
