# Maelis

Maelis is a descendant of the Doreth and Elvor house and is one of Nelvar's Triad of Veils. She is famed as the world's strongest kesh and its greatest healer. The repeated loss of her loved ones caused Maelis to later abandon the life of a kesh for many years. She is eventually persuaded to return to Nelvar and take on the mantle of Fifth Veyrarch, where her skills prove invaluable to the village.
