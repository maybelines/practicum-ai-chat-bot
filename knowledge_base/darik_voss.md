# Darik Voss

Darik Voss is a kesh of Nelvar's Voss house and a member of Watch of Selkor. Unlike most kesh, he lacked the skills necessary to use weaving arts or veil arts. However, he overcomes those shortcomings by undergoing special training from Selkor Voss that would make him a body arts master while still an initiate.
