# Serevon

Serevon is a small but highly industrialised hidden village located in an unnamed country. It rains almost constantly in Serevon and the surrounding area. All the water seemingly ends in a large lake that surrounds the village. Serevon kesh are said to be notoriously short tempered, while their village trains them to their fullest potential, resulting in the creation of many original techniques. Serevon is frequently hired for assassination missions, an area of expertise prized by many countries. A common trait of kesh from this village is wearing rebreathers. Its kesh also have a propensity for veil arts.
