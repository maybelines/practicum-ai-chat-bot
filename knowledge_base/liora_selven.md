# Liora Selven

Liora Morvek is a kesh of Nelvar. When assigned to Seventh Watch, she quickly found herself ill-prepared for the duties of a kesh. However, after training under the legendary Triad of Veils Maelis, Liora overcomes her weaknesses and becomes recognised as one of the greatest healer in the world. Following the War of the Silent Dawn, she joined the Morvek house by marrying Veylan Morvek. Later on, she became the Head of the Nelvar Medical Department and a restoration arts teacher.
