# Mireth Sight

The Mireth Sight is the inherited sight gift of the Morvek house that appears selectively among its members. It is regarded as one of the "Three Sight Gifts", the others being the Auralis Sight and the Selenor Sight. While its powers originated from Vesnara Arven's Selenor Crown, its independent form was first manifested by Vaerion Arven.
