# Roven Morvek

Roven Morvek was a member of Nelvar's Morvek house. He was believed to have died during the War of the Broken Gate, his only surviving legacy being the Mireth Sight he gave to his teammate, Nerik Valdor. In truth, Roven was saved from death and trained by Mordain, but the events of the war left Roven disillusioned with reality, and he inherited Mordain's plan to create an ideal world. Resurfacing under the names of Velsh and Mordain Morvek himself, Roven subtly took control of the Circle of Veskar, using them as a means to advance his machinations, eventually going public and starting the War of the Silent Dawn. However, towards the war's conclusion, Roven had a change of heart and, as atonement, sacrificed his life to save the same world he sought to replace.
