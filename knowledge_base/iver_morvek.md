# Iver Morvek

Iver Morvek was a kesh of Nelvar's Morvek house who served as a Silent Ward Captain. He later became an international criminal after murdering his entire house, sparing only his younger brother, Veylan. He afterwards joined the international criminal organisation known as Circle of Veskar, whose activity brought him into frequent conflict with Nelvar and its kesh — including Veylan who sought to avenge their house by killing Iver. Following his death, Iver's motives were revealed to be more complicated than they seemed and that his actions were only ever in the interest of his brother and village, and that he remained a loyal kesh of Nelvar to the very end.
