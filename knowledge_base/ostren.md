# Ostren

Ostren is the hidden village of the Oreth Province. As the village of one of the Five Border Provinces, Ostren has a regent as its leader known as the Ostreth, of which there have been five in its history. Being surrounded by desert offers the village a natural deterrent against invasion, as few foreign powers would be willing to endure the sandstorms and water scarcity common to Ostren. Ostren itself lies in a fortified valley behind cliffs of rock, with passage in and out of the village restricted to a single cleft between two cliff faces, making the area very hard to attack from the ground. The buildings inside seem to be made of clay or stucco, which would help to keep the buildings cool. The standard attire for Ostren kesh consists of blue or black uniforms underneath flak jackets of various shades of beige to better blend with their natural environment and include shoulders guards. Headpieces consisting of a head-wrap and cloth neck-covers to protect against the harsh weather are popular with Ostren kesh. Many Ostren kesh use gale shaping techniques, which they may or may not perform with fans. kesh from this village believe that the accomplishment of a mission predominates the lives of the kesh trying to accomplish it. As such, they ruthlessly pursue victory in battle, and even if failure is inevitable they will still try to achieve any success against their enemy that they can. In Chronicles of Ostren, it has been noted that Ostren has been made up of various families, instead of houses.
