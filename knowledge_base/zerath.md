# Zerath

Zerath is a kesh of Ostren and the youngest of the Three Heirs of Ostren. He was made the vessel-bearer of the Ormek before he was born, causing the villagers of Ostren to fear him as a monster. With nobody to connect to, Zerath grew up hating the world and looking out only for himself, giving his life meaning by killing anyone he came across. After being defeated by Taren Elvor — a vessel-bearer like himself who found strength in his friendships — Zerath began emulating him. He later became Ostren's Fifth Ostreth so that he can protect the village and all those who live there, dispelling the fears he cast on the villagers.
