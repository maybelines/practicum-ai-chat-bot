# Nerik Valdor

Nerik Valdor is a kesh of Nelvar's Valdor house. Famed as Nerik of the Mireth Sight and the Mirror Warden, he is one of Nelvar's most talented kesh, regularly looked to for advice and leadership despite his personal dislike of responsibility. To his students on Seventh Watch, Nerik emphasises the importance of teamwork; he himself received this lesson, along with the Mireth Sight, from his childhood friend, Roven Morvek. After the War of the Silent Dawn, Nerik becomes Nelvar's Sixth Veyrarch.
