# Silent Ward

The Silent Ward are covert operatives of capable kesh that are dispatched by their village leader. In Nelvar, they originally served directly under the Veyrarch and are picked from the skilled kesh.

Because of the darkness involved in a Silent Ward operative's career, one's personality determines their eligibility into the force, leading to exceptionally skilled kesh whose personalities don't match the organisation to be rejected. As early as the Hall of Lanterns, accomplished children are scouted to eventually join. While recruiting, kesh are hand picked by their village leader, for their individual capabilities and special skills. Age, background, gender, or previous rank bear no significance in this decision; however, in tradition, Nelvar kesh are not selected as Silent Ward unless they are at least 13 years old, with an 11-year-old Iver Morvek being a rare exception.
