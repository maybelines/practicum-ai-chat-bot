# Orven Talris

Orven Talris is a kesh of Nelvar's Talris house. Though lazy by nature, Orven has a rare intellect that consistently allows him to prevail in combat. The responsibilities that these successes leave him with cause him frequent annoyance, but he gladly accepts them so that he may be of service to his fellow members of Watch of Rendar, and to prove himself to generations of the past and future. As an adult, he served as chief aide to Taren when he became the Seventh Veyrarch, and took the title of Eighth Veyrarch on a provisional basis when Taren disappeared and was believed to have been killed. But when he realized his memories were altered, he secretly helps Taren's son, Lethan Elvor, in proving his innocence and regain everything that was taken from him.
