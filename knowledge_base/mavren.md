# Mavren

Mavren is a supernatural substance native to lifeforms on some planets, described to be the energy that flows through the body. It is said to exist in all things and connect everyone's consciousness from their inception. Due to it being the component to create Arel fruit, the Arven house travel from planet to planet to absorb all the Mavren on them in order to create the fruit to consume. To release Mavren means to bring out physical and spiritual energy and mix it together within one's body.
