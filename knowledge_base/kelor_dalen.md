# Kelor Dalen

Kelor Dalen was a kesh of Nelvar's Dalen house. Though a prodigy even by the Dalen's standards, Kelor was a member of the house's branch house; no matter how skilled he became, he would always be in service to the Dalen's main house, a fact that convinced him fate was predetermined. After experiencing Taren Elvor's refusal to be limited by such an ideology, Kelor realised his fate was what he chose it to be, and as a member of Watch of Selkor he sought the strength necessary to make the future he wanted for his family and friends.
