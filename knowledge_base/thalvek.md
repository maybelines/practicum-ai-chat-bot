# Thalvek

Thalvek is the hidden village of the Teral Province and was founded by the First Thalarch. As the village of one of the Five Border Provinces, Thalvek has a regent as its leader known as the Thalarch, of which there have been five in its history. The village is located in a range of tall mountains, and is literally hidden in the clouds. The Thalarch works in a large blue structure built into the tallest mountain. The standard attire for Thalvek kesh consists of a long, grey top which gathers just at the waist to give a sash-like appearance, with a matching coloured bottom. Over this they wear white, one-strapped flak jackets and arm as well as shin-guards. Many Thalvek kesh use spark shaping techniques.
