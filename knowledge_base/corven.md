# Corven

Corven was one of Nelvar's Triad of Veils. Famed as a hermit and pervert of stupendous kesh skill, Corven travelled the world in search of knowledge that would help his friends, the various novels he wrote, and, posthumously, the world in its entirety – knowledge that would be passed on to his godson and final student, Taren Elvor.
