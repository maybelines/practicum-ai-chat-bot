# Taren Elvor

Taren Elvor is a kesh of Nelvar's Elvor house and a reincarnation and descendant of Thariel Arven. He became the bearer of Irmoran on the day of his birth — a fate that caused him to be shunned by most of Nelvar throughout his childhood. After joining Watch of Nerik, Taren worked hard to gain the village's acknowledgement all the while chasing his dream to become Veyrarch. In the following years, through many hardships and ordeals, he became a capable kesh, regarded as a hero both by the villagers, and soon after, the rest of the world, becoming known as the Guardian of Nelvar. He soon proved to be one of the main factors in winning the War of the Silent Dawn, leading him to achieve his dream and become the village's Seventh Veyrarch.
