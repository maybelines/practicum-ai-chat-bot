# Auralis Sight

The Auralis Sight is an inherited sight gift that originated from the Arven house. After the Arven settled on Elvar, the Auralis Sight became more commonly associated with the Dalen house, direct descendants of the Arven. It is regarded as one of the "Three Sight Gifts", the others being the Mireth Sight and the Selenor Sight.
