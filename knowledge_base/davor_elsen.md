# Davor Elsen

Davor Elsen was the Fourth Veyrarch of Nelvar. He was renowned and feared all over the world, across multiple nations as the Swift Light of Nelvar. He died during the Breach of the Night Gate, sacrificing his life to seal a half of the Irmoran into himself and the other half into his newborn son, Taren Elvor.
