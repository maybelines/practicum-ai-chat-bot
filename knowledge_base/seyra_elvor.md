# Seyra Elvor

Seyra Elvor was a Nelvar kesh who originated from Valestra's Elvor house. She was the second bearer of Irmoran.

Born into the famed Elvor house of Valestra, Seyra lived most of her youth being caught up in war, making her seek for daily peace. At a young age, she was sent to Nelvar and enrolled in the Hall of Lanterns after the destruction of her village. Wanting to make a strong first impression, she proclaimed to her class that she would be the first female Veyrarch. The other kids laughed at her statement and began calling her "Red Lantern"because of her then-round face and fiery red hair.
