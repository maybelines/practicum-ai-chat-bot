# Veyra Dalen

Veyra Elvor is a kesh of Nelvar. Formerly the heiress of the Dalen house, she lost the position upon being deemed unsuited for the responsibilities of leading the house. Nonetheless, Veyra persevered and from her observation of Taren Elvor especially, Veyra found an example to follow. Through her membership with Eighth Watch, she sought to become strong enough to change herself, if even a little at a time. A few years after the War of the Silent Dawn, Veyra joined the Elvor house after marrying Taren.
