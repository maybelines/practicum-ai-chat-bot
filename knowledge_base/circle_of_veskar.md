# Circle of Veskar

Circle of Veskar was a group of kesh that existed outside the usual system of the hidden villages. Over the course of several decades, Circle of Veskar took different forms and was led by different individuals. Though each iteration is viewed as either subversives or criminals, all sought to make the world a better place through their own means. Each Circle of Veskar tended to have multiple lairs across the world, inaccessible either due to their remoteness or the various security measures that protected them.
