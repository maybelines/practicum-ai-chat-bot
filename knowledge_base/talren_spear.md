# Talren Spear

The Talren Spear is a high concentration of lightning Mavren channelled around the user's hand.

The Talren Spear was created by Nerik Valdor after he failed to apply his spark affinity to the Orveth Sphere. He intended for the Talren Spear to be a weapon used only to protect friends and loved ones. To perform, the user first induces vital surge or greater vital surge which affords the production of immense Mavren, allowing them to gather lightning to their hand; the high concentration of electricity produces a sound reminiscent of many birds chirping. The lightning Mavren has been shown on occasions to damage the user's hand, peeling the skin and causing it to smoulder slightly, but this does not appear to actually be debilitating.
